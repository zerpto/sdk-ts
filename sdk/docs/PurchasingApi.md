## createBillFromPurchaseOrder

> ApInvoiceResponse createBillFromPurchaseOrder(id, billFromPurchaseOrderInput, idempotencyKey)

Create a bill from a PO

One bill can cover several goods receipts of this PO (all unbilled posted receipts, or the listed ones). For receipts across several POs of one vendor, use &#x60;POST /v1/purchasing/bills&#x60;. The bill is a normal AP bill (&#x60;/v1/ap/invoices&#x60;) in &#x60;draft&#x60;: edit it with &#x60;PATCH /v1/ap/invoices/{id}&#x60; until it is posted. Its lines are pre-filled from the receipt lines at the PO price, so it can differ from what was received (quantity, price, extra lines) as the vendor invoice does. Each receipt can be on one bill only, and each uploaded document can back one bill only. Error codes: &#x60;receipt_not_posted&#x60;, &#x60;receipt_already_billed&#x60;, &#x60;document_already_used&#x60;, &#x60;vendor_mismatch&#x60;, &#x60;nothing_to_bill&#x60;.  **Response meta** - &#x60;meta&#x60; is empty.

## postPurchaseReturn

> PurchaseReturnResponse postPurchaseReturn(id, idempotencyKey)

Post purchase return

Takes the stock out at the cost it came in at (not the current average) and posts debit GRNI, credit inventory. Received quantity on the PO is reduced by the returned quantity, so a replacement delivery is a new goods receipt on the same PO. If the receipt is already billed, a draft AP credit note is created (&#x60;credit_note_id&#x60;) for the returned value; review and post it with &#x60;POST /v1/ap/credit-notes/{id}/post&#x60;. If it is not billed yet, the open GRNI for the receipt is reduced and no credit note is needed. Fails with 422 if the period is closed.
