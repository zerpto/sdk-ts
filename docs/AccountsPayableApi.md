## allocateApPayment

> ApPaymentResponse allocateApPayment(id, allocationsInput, idempotencyKey)

Apply payment to bills

Used for down payments (a payment created without allocations, usually with &#x60;purchase_order_id&#x60;) and for any payment with a balance left. The total applied cannot exceed the unallocated balance (&#x60;exceeds_unallocated&#x60;), each amount cannot exceed the bill\&#39;s &#x60;amount_due&#x60; (&#x60;exceeds_amount_due&#x60;), and the bills must be posted and belong to the payment\&#39;s vendor (&#x60;vendor_mismatch&#x60;). Posts debit AP, credit vendor advances for the applied amount. Fails with 422 if the period is closed.  **Response meta** - &#x60;meta&#x60; is empty.
